Our Engineering Philosophy: True Component-Level Repair

When an industrial VFD or automation card fails, OEM service representatives typically recommend replacing the entire complete unit. This creates astronomical costs, import delays of 4 to 8 weeks, and unnecessary electronic waste.

At Repair Q Electronics, we isolate the exact micro-components that have failed — such as shorted IGBT modules, degraded gate optocouplers, dry electrolytic capacitor banks, blown switching power supply ICs, or damaged RS-485 transceiver chips. By replacing only the damaged components with high-grade equivalents, we restore the original drive to factory operating parameters at a fraction of the cost.

Laboratory Bench Diagnostic Infrastructure

Our workshop in Jhajjar is equipped with specialized instruments specifically designed for high-power electronics troubleshooting:

  • Digital Storage Oscilloscopes (DSO): Used for checking high-frequency PWM switching symmetry across upper and lower gate driver circuits.
  • Dynamic Motor Load Test Rig: A dedicated bench testing motor loaded dynamically to verify torque stability, current draw, and thermal performance under realistic operating stress.
  • Infrared Thermal Imaging: Locating microscopic circuit hot-spots, leaking SMD ceramic capacitors, and uneven IGBT thermal transfer.
  • Micro-Soldering & Hot-Air Rework Stations: Precision desoldering of multi-pin surface-mount devices (SMD), QFP microprocessors, and high-current PCB copper busbars.
  • Insulation & ESR Testers: High-voltage Megger insulation resistance testing and in-circuit equivalent series resistance (ESR) capacitor health analysis.
