Key Industries & Equipment Running in Delhi Industrial Belts

Delhi's diverse manufacturing pockets rely on industrial electronics across multiple sectors:

  • Bawana & Narela: Plastic injection moulding machines (Techmation boards, HRTC cards, servo hydraulic VFDs), blow moulding, packaging lines, and wire drawing machines.
  • Mayapuri & Wazirpur: Metal stamping, sheet metal press brakes (Cybelec, Delem controllers), steel rolling mills, and CNC machine shops.
  • Okhla Industrial Area (Phases I, II, III): Pharmaceutical packaging, FMCG processing, commercial printing presses, and commercial building HVAC chillers (Carrier, Trane, York).
  • Mundka & Nangloi: Footwear manufacturing, corrugated box production, and conveyor drive automation.

Frequently Asked Questions (Delhi Industrial Belts)

Do you have a physical workshop in Delhi?

No. Our physical repair laboratory is exclusively located in Jhajjar, Haryana. We serve Delhi factories through rapid door-to-door courier, scheduled factory pickup, and direct on-site factory visits.

How do Delhi factories send heavy VFDs or machines cards to your Jhajjar lab?

For units under 25 kg, factories commonly use fast couriers (BlueDart, DTDC, Trackon). For bulky heavy-horsepower drives, we coordinate direct factory pickup or you can drop it off at our workshop near Yadav Dharamshala, Circular Road, Jhajjar.
