Common VFD Faults & Error Codes We Fix

Factory environments subject AC drives to heavy thermal cycles, voltage surges, conductive carbon dust, and phase imbalances. Our component-level diagnostics target the exact root causes of critical trips:

Fault Code / Symptom Underlying Root Cause Component-Level Repair Solution
Overcurrent (OC / F0001 / AL01) Punctured IGBT junction, shorted output phase, gate driver diode leakage. Replace IGBT bridge, rebuild 6-channel gate drive resistors and optocouplers.
Overvoltage (OV / DC Bus High) Failed braking chopper transistor, defective DC bus voltage divider sensing circuit. Replace dynamic braking IGBT, calibrate high-precision voltage sensing op-amps.
Undervoltage (UV / Phase Loss) Input bridge rectifier diode degradation, dry DC capacitor bank, open pre-charge relay. Replace 3-phase input rectifier module, test ESR of electrolytic capacitor bank.
Ground / Earth Fault (GF / EF) Current sensor (Hall effect sensor) drift, insulation breakdown on output traces. Re-calibrate or replace Hall current sensors, apply conformal insulating coating.
No Display / Dead Power Supply Blown primary SMPS switching IC (TOP/VIPer), shorted auxiliary transformer winding. Rebuild 24V/15V/5V SMPS control circuit, replace secondary filter capacitors.

Our 4-Stage Precision VFD Repair Protocol

  1. Cold Circuit Safety & Static Diagnostic: Comprehensive diode check on 3-phase input rectifier bridge and 6-pulse IGBT inverter stage. DC bus impedance analysis to prevent secondary short circuits upon power-up.
  2. Micro-Soldering & Component Replacement: High-temperature vacuum desoldering of damaged power modules. Replacement with OEM-grade IGBT modules, matched gate resistors, and isolated gate drivers (HCPL/TLP optocouplers).
  3. Gate Waveform Balancing on Oscilloscope: Verification of symmetric PWM square waves across all upper and lower gate channels before applying high voltage to the DC link.
  4. Dynamic Motor Load Rig Testing: Every drive is tested under continuous mechanical load coupled to our lab test motor. Current draw, thermal dissipation, ramp-up stability, and reverse torque are verified and documented.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a blown IGBT module in a VFD be repaired without replacing the whole drive?

Yes. We desolder the damaged module, rebuild the gate driver resistors and isolation chips, and install a brand-new, thermally balanced power module. The drive operates with original factory performance at a fraction of the cost.

How do you test large horsepower drives?

We test drives up to 500 HP using our dynamic motor test rig, verifying current draw, thermal profile under infrared thermography, and regenerative braking chopper operation.
