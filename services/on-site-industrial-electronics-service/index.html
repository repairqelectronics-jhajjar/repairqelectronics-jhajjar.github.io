  • Plastic & Moulding Plants: Techmation machines, servo hydraulic pumps, HRTC temperature issues
  • Automobile & Auto-Ancillary: VFD conveyor lines, robotic welding drive faults, PLC I/O failures
  • Textile & Packaging Units: High-speed winding drive synchronizations, multi-drive DC bus networks
  • Heavy Engineering & Fabrication: Press brake CNC positioning, crane VFD slip ring conversions

Common Faults & Micro-Diagnostic Solutions

Breakdown Scenario On-Site Investigation Method Action Taken
New Drive Trips Instantly on Power-Up Insulation resistance (Megger) test on motor windings, line harmonic check. Isolate motor winding defect or configure drive acceleration & voltage boost.
Intermittent Communication Loss on Fieldbus Oscilloscope check on RS-485 / Profibus lines for missing termination resistors. Install 120-ohm terminating resistors, replace damaged transceiver nodes.
Heavy Blown VFD / PLC Power Unit Safe de-coupling of power cables, tag-out, and initial damage assessment. Carefully remove unit for precision bench repair at our Jhajjar laboratory.

Our Bench Repair & Rig Testing Procedure

  1. Immediate Technical Call: Call Ajay directly at 8307193825 to discuss error codes, machine type, and line stoppage urgency.
  2. On-Site Factory Dispatch: We arrive at your plant equipped with digital oscilloscopes, insulation testers, multimeters, and parameter consoles.
  3. Safe Disconnection & Lab Bench Repair: If component replacement is needed, we safely extract the unit and transport it to our Jhajjar lab.
  4. Re-Installation & Commissioning: Re-mounting the repaired unit, reconnecting field wiring, and conducting a live motor load run.
