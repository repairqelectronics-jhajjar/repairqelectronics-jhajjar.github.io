Common Faults & Micro-Diagnostic Solutions

Fault Symptom Probable Cause Component-Level Fix
Phase Short Circuit / Blown Thyristor Heavy inrush surge, lightning transient, thermal breakdown of SCR junction. Replace anti-parallel SCR module, verify snubber network RC circuit.
Bypass Contactor Failure / Contact Welding High motor start duty cycle, arcing contacts, stuck mechanical linkage. Rebuild auxiliary bypass contact assembly or replace contactor driver relay.
Gate Firing Missing / Motor Won't Ramp Pulse transformer degradation, blown gate drive opto-triac or transistor. Rebuild firing pulse train circuit, verify gate voltage and rise time on scope.
Thermal Overload Trip During Startup Internal thermistor drift, cooling fan failure, heatsink thermal paste drying. Re-paste power stack with thermal compound, replace fan & calibrate PTC thermistor.

Our Bench Repair & Rig Testing Procedure

  1. High-Voltage Isolation & SCR Static Test: Measurement of forward blocking voltage, reverse leakage current, and gate-to-cathode resistance across all 6 SCRs.
  2. Gate Pulse Firing Scope Analysis: Verification of firing pulse angle (35° to 180°), firing symmetry, and gate trigger current across all three phases.
  3. Bypass Mechanism & Snubber Circuit Overhaul: Cleaning, testing, and calibrating internal bypass contactors and replacing worn metal oxide varistors (MOVs).
  4. Ramp-Up & Current Limit Testing: Verification of soft start voltage ramp, current limit thresholds, and soft stop deceleration profiles under controlled load.
