Courier & Transport Guidelines

If you are shipping equipment via courier from out-of-area plants:

  • Accepted Couriers: BlueDart, DTDC, Delhivery, Trackon, and local transport logistics.
  • Packaging Advice: Wrap electronic cards in anti-static ESD bubble wrap and pack securely with high-density foam cushioning.
  • Included Documentation: Please enclose a job sheet stating the observed error code, input voltage, and contact person phone number.
